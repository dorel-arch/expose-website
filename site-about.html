The Origin

One freelancer, one broken system.

Dorel Shnaiderman spent years as a working creative — shooting campaigns, designing brands, chasing down clients for payments that never came on time. He watched brilliant people go unnoticed while average work got pushed by the right agencies. The unfairness wasn't subtle. It was baked in.

So instead of complaining about it, he started building. The first version of EXPOSE was just a tool to help him manage his own workflow. Then his friends wanted it. Then strangers started asking. That's when he knew — this wasn't just his problem.

"EXPOSE is for the creative who deserves better, the brand that wants more, and the agency that needs to move faster."

D
Dorel Shnaiderman Co-Founder & CEO

And we're just getting started.

Why We Exist

Three things we refuse to accept.

Fair pay for real work

Creatives should charge what their work is worth — and get paid for it. Not haggle with scope-creeping clients or wait 90 days for an invoice to clear.

Talent deserves to be seen

Visibility shouldn't be a privilege for people with big followings or industry connections. The right brief should find the right creator — every time.

Marketing should feel human

We help creatives present themselves authentically. No cringe-worthy self-promotion. Just the right message, to the right people, at the right time.
